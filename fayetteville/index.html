How Fayetteville Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement.

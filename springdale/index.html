Here's what we see most often when something breaks at home: an air conditioner blowing warm air, a condenser that hums but won't start, a furnace that lights for a few seconds and quits, a breaker that keeps tripping, an evaporator coil that has frozen, a condensate drain backed up into the furnace cabinet, or a thermostat that has quit. Two common situations call for a quick step from you before our technician gets there. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: switch the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or gives off a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. That small step can prevent a much larger repair.

Keeping Your Springdale HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored through a full season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
